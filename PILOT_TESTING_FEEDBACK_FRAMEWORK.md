# PHASE 3C: PILOT TESTING & FEEDBACK COLLECTION FRAMEWORK

## The Manager's Field Guide — Pilot Testing Strategy

---

## PART 1: PILOT TESTING OVERVIEW

### Objectives
1. **Test Content Clarity:** Verify that chapters are understandable to target audience (branch/team managers)
2. **Test Practical Applicability:** Ensure exercises and scripts work in real manager scenarios
3. **Test Training Format:** Validate that 23-session curriculum is right length, pacing, and structure
4. **Test Impact:** Measure whether managers actually change behavior and improve team performance
5. **Gather Feedback:** Collect structured input for refinement before full rollout

### Pilot Duration
- **Phase 1 (Weeks 1-4):** Small group testing (5-8 managers)
- **Phase 2 (Weeks 5-8):** Medium group expansion (15-20 managers)
- **Phase 3 (Weeks 9-12):** Impact measurement and refinement
- **Decision Point:** Go/No-Go for full rollout

### Success Criteria
- [ ] 80%+ of participants rate content as "useful" or "highly useful"
- [ ] 70%+ complete all 23 sessions
- [ ] Facilitators report exercises are executable
- [ ] Managers report using at least 3 frameworks/scripts in actual work
- [ ] Team performance metrics show improvement (attendance, engagement, turnover)
- [ ] Feedback indicates ≤5 critical content changes needed

## PART 2: PHASE 1 — SMALL GROUP PILOT (5-8 MANAGERS)

### Selection Criteria for Pilot Group

**Target Profile:**
- Branch managers with 3-10 direct reports
- Mix of experience levels (first-time to 10+ years)
- Willingness to apply content and give feedback
- Geographic diversity (3-4 branches minimum)
- Balanced: 3-4 newer managers, 2-3 experienced managers

**Exclusion Criteria:**
- Managers in crisis situations (turnover, compliance issues)
- Managers known to be resistant to change
- Managers planning to leave within 6 months

### Recruitment Approach
1. **Identify candidates** through HR/Regional Manager
2. **Pitch clearly:** "Help us test a new manager development program. We need your honest feedback."
3. **Secure commitment:** 2 hours/week for 6 months, complete all sessions
4. **Provide incentive:** Certificate of completion, access to advanced materials first

### Phase 1 Structure

**Delivery Format:**
- Weekly 90-minute sessions on Friday afternoon (non-business-critical time)
- Hybrid: In-person preferred, virtual option for remote branches
- Same facilitator (consistent experience)
- Max group size: 8 (intimate, high-engagement setting)

**Session Rhythm:**
- **Week 1-4:** Chapters 1-7 (Start With Yourself through Culture Building)
- **Week 5-8:** Chapters 8-13 (Hiring through Know Your People)
- **Week 9-14:** Chapters 14-23 (Develop People through Performance Management)
- **Week 15-16:** Integration and Advanced Topics
- **Week 17-24:** Sustainability check-ins (bi-weekly)

**Facilitator Role:**
- Teach chapter content
- Guide exercises
- Answer questions
- Observe which parts resonate vs. confuse
- Take detailed notes on participant reactions

### Phase 1 Feedback Mechanisms

**During Sessions (Real-Time):**
- Observe engagement levels and note confusion points
- Ask "What's unclear?" after each exercise
- Track which scripts participants want to use immediately
- Note questions they ask

**After Each Session (5 min):**
- Quick poll: "How useful was this session? (1-5 scale)"
- Open: "What will you try this week?"
- Barrier: "What might prevent you from using this?"

**Monthly (Mid-Pilot Conversations):**
- 30-minute 1-on-1 with each participant
- **Questions:**
  * "What frameworks have you used?"
  * "What worked? What didn't?"
  * "What's confusing?"
  * "What's missing?"
  * "How is your team responding?"

**End-of-Phase Survey (Week 8):**
Detailed feedback form covering:
- Content clarity (each part rated separately)
- Practical usefulness (frameworks, scripts, exercises)
- Pacing and timing
- Facilitator effectiveness
- What should change
- What to keep exactly as is

**Observation Metrics:**
- Attendance rate (target: 90%+)
- Participation in exercises (active vs. passive)
- Questions asked (engagement indicator)
- Scripts brought to facilitator ("I used this with my team and...")
- Early implementation signs

### Phase 1 Deliverables

**From Participants:**
- Completed feedback forms (quantitative + qualitative)
- Written reflections on behavior change
- Real examples of framework application
- Documented barriers to implementation

**From Facilitator:**
- Session observation notes (what worked, what confused)
- Audio/video recording of sessions (with permission) for review
- Facilitator reflection: "How did I have to adjust?"
- Compiled feedback summary

**Content Adjustments:**
- Document every piece of feedback
- Identify themes (e.g., "Chapter 10 interview questions felt too long")
- Categorize: Must fix, Should improve, Nice to have
- Create revision list for Phase 2

## PART 3: PHASE 2 — MEDIUM GROUP EXPANSION (15-20 MANAGERS)

### What Changes from Phase 1

**Based on Phase 1 feedback:**
- Revise chapters that were confusing
- Adjust pacing if needed
- Improve exercises based on what worked
- Simplify or expand scripts based on facilitator experience
- Update curriculum with timing adjustments

**Expanded Group:**
- 2-3 parallel cohorts (5-7 per group)
- 2-3 facilitators (allows comparison of delivery approaches)
- More geographic spread
- More experience level diversity

### Phase 2 Structure

**Delivery:**
- Same 90-minute weekly format
- Cohorts run in parallel (allows peer comparison)
- Different facilitators (tests content robustness vs. facilitator style)
- Mix of in-person and virtual

**Feedback Collection (More Rigorous):**

**Weekly Post-Session Pulse (2 minutes):**
```
Session #__: [Chapter Name]

1. Clarity: 1=confusing  5=very clear          [1  2  3  4  5]
2. Usefulness: 1=not useful  5=very useful    [1  2  3  4  5]
3. Exercise: 1=hard  5=easy to do             [1  2  3  4  5]
4. Will I use this? 1=no  5=definitely yes    [1  2  3  4  5]

Quick note: What will you try this week?
```

**Bi-Weekly Deep Dives (20 minutes):**
- Small group (2-3 participants) + facilitator
- "Show me what you've tried"
- "Where did you get stuck?"
- "What would make this more useful?"

**Monthly Behavior Tracking:**
- Facilitator interviews: "What behavior changes are you seeing?"
- Peer accountability pairs: "How is your accountability partner using this?"
- Manager reflections: Written response to "I applied this framework with..."

**Mid-Phase Assessment (Week 8):**
- Formal survey (similar to Phase 1 but refined based on feedback)
- Facilitator roundtable: Comparative notes from all facilitators
- Participant focus group: 60-minute deep conversation

**End-of-Phase Full Assessment (Week 16):**

**Quantitative:**
- Content clarity scores by chapter
- Usefulness ratings by framework/section
- Pacing and timing assessment
- Facilitator effectiveness comparison

**Qualitative:**
- Open feedback: What to change, what to keep
- Real-world examples: How each framework was used
- Barrier identification: What prevented implementation
- Benefit evidence: Team changes observed

**Behavioral Metrics:**
- Scripts actually used (which ones most, which never?)
- Frameworks applied (Capability Matrix? Results+Relationships? Feedback model?)
- Exercises tried (which ones led to behavior change?)
- Actions taken (difficult conversations had? Feedback given? Recognition systems started?)

### Phase 2 Outputs

**Refined Content:**
- Chapter revisions based on feedback
- Improved exercises (clearer instructions, better examples)
- Clarified scripts (shorter, more usable)
- Expanded appendices if needed

**Facilitator Training Materials:**
- Facilitator guide with "what to watch for" in each chapter
- Answers to common questions
- Tips for exercises that tend to confuse
- How to help without giving away learning

**Curriculum Refinements:**
- Adjusted session timing
- Reordered content if needed
- Added bridges between concepts
- Improved transitions between chapters

## PART 4: PHASE 3 — IMPACT MEASUREMENT (WEEKS 9-12)

### What We Measure

**Behavior Change (Manager Level):**
- Did they actually use the frameworks?
- Did they change how they manage?
- Did they start doing things they weren't doing before?

**Measurement Approach:**

**1. Self-Reported Usage (Manager Reflection):**
```
Framework Usage Tracker (Monthly):

[ ] Capability × Commitment Matrix — plotted team members
[ ] Results + Relationships culture — clarity conversation
[ ] ASK Framework — used in hiring/role design
[ ] Feedback conversation — gave feedback using framework
[ ] 1-on-1 meetings — established regular cadence
[ ] Delegation — handed off work to develop people
[ ] Recognition — actively recognized performance
[ ] Difficult conversation — addressed performance/fit issue
[ ] Appraisal — conducted formal review
[ ] Weekly Operating System — used manager planning framework

Notes: Where did these work? Where did they fail?
```

**2. Behavioral Observation (From Facilitator/Manager):**
- Are they talking about the frameworks in sessions?
- Are they bringing real scenarios to apply?
- Are they asking follow-up questions?
- Are they frustrated or engaged?
- What language are they using? (adopted framework language?)

**3. Team Impact (360 Input):**

**Team Member Survey (Anonymous):**
```
About Your Manager (1=Strongly Disagree to 5=Strongly Agree):

1. My manager clearly explains what success looks like
2. My manager recognizes good work
3. My manager gives me feedback to help me improve
4. I understand how my work connects to team goals
5. My manager trusts me to make decisions
6. My manager helps me develop new skills
7. My manager is fair in how they treat people
8. I feel valued on this team
9. I would recommend this manager to others
10. I feel more developed than I did 6 months ago

Additional: What's one thing your manager did well this quarter?
What's one thing your manager could improve?
```

**Manager Self-Assessment (Before/After Program):**
- Compare self-assessment (Appendix 1) from Week 1 vs. Week 24
- Areas of most change?
- Confidence improvement?

**4. Business Metrics:**

**Team-Level Indicators (Compare 3 months before to 3 months after):**
- Attendance/absenteeism
- Turnover (people leaving the team)
- New hire success rate (promoted from team)
- Customer satisfaction (if applicable)
- Loan approvals/closures (if sales role)
- Internal promotions (manager developing people for next level)
- Complaints/escalations (declining as issues handled early)

**Performance Metrics:**
- Individual goal achievement on team
- Quality indicators
- Relationship/satisfaction metrics

### Phase 3 Data Collection

**Month 1 (Week 9-12) — Baseline:**
- Self-assessments (manager + team survey)
- Business metrics snapshot
- Facilitator observation summary

**Month 2 (Week 13-16) — Mid-Point:**
- Team survey pulse (abbreviated)
- Facilitator interviews: "What's changing?"
- Manager reflections: Documented usage

**Month 3 (Week 17-20) — Final:**
- Full team survey (same as baseline)
- Manager self-assessment repeat
- Business metrics update
- Comprehensive feedback from facilitators

### Phase 3 Analysis Framework

**For Each Manager:**
- Which frameworks did they adopt?
- Which ones are they avoiding?
- What's the barrier for non-adoption?
- What behavior changed?
- What improved on their team?
- What was the biggest insight for them?

**Aggregate Findings:**
- Which chapters/frameworks were most impactful?
- Which had the least adoption (why)?
- Which exercises were most useful?
- Which scripts were actually used?
- What's the ROI on manager time investment?
- Facilitator quality assessment: Did style matter?

**Success Indicators:**
- High satisfaction + High adoption + Team improvement = Content validated
- High satisfaction + Low adoption + No team improvement = Script clarity issue
- Medium satisfaction + High adoption + Team improvement = Manager self-selection bias (wanted to change anyway)
- Low satisfaction + Low adoption + No change = Content doesn't resonate for this group (explore why)

## PART 5: FEEDBACK INTEGRATION & GO/NO-GO DECISION

### Compilation Meeting (After Week 20)

**Participants:**
- HR Director
- Training lead/Facilitators
- Pilot manager representatives (2-3)
- Author/Content owner

**Review:**
- Quantitative results: Satisfaction, adoption, impact
- Qualitative themes: What changed, what didn't, why
- Facilitator insights: What worked, what broke down
- Cost-benefit: Investment vs. return

### Decision Criteria

**GO (Full Rollout) Requires:**
- ✅ 75%+ satisfaction with content
- ✅ 60%+ adoption of key frameworks
- ✅ Visible team impact in at least 60% of pilot group
- ✅ Facilitators confident in delivery
- ✅ <5 critical content changes needed
- ✅ Business leader buy-in

**CONDITIONAL GO (Rollout with Modifications) Requires:**
- 65-75% satisfaction
- 50-60% adoption
- Impact in 40-60% of group
- Clear action plan for specific improvements
- Timeline for updates before wider rollout

**NO-GO / PAUSE:**
- <65% satisfaction
- <50% adoption
- Facilitator concerns about core content
- >10 critical issues identified
- Business impact not evident
- Recommendation: Pause, revise, re-pilot

### Revisions Before Rollout

**If GO Decision:**
- Minor refinements based on feedback
- Facilitator training on lessons learned
- Create "common questions" FAQ
- Prepare org communication for rollout

**If CONDITIONAL GO:**
- 2-week content revision sprint
- Facilitator training refresh
- Small follow-up test (1-2 chapters)
- Revised facilitator guide

**If NO-GO:**
- Comprehensive content review
- Determine: Is this the right content for this audience?
- Redesign problem areas
- Plan re-pilot in 8-12 weeks

## PART 6: FULL ROLLOUT PLAN (IF GO DECISION)

### Scaling from Pilot to Organization

**Timeline:**
- **Months 1-2:** Train all facilitators (regional leads, HR partners)
- **Months 3-4:** Cohort 1 (40-50 managers)
- **Months 5-6:** Cohort 2 (40-50 managers)
- **Months 7-8:** Cohort 3 (remaining managers)

**Facilitator Preparation:**
- 2-day facilitator training (content + delivery)
- Observation of pilot facilitators (live sessions)
- Practice teaching with peer feedback
- FAQ and troubleshooting guide

**Rollout Communication:**
- Letter from CEO: Why this matters
- Manager invitation: What it is, why you're selected, what to expect
- Team communication: Why your manager is in this program, how it benefits you
- Monthly updates: Progress, success stories, impact data

**Support Structures:**
- Facilitator hot-line for questions
- Monthly facilitator sync-ups (share experiences, problem-solve)
- Accountability pairs (managers support each other between sessions)
- Optional "advanced topics" sessions after core curriculum

**Sustainability:**
- Quarterly manager forums: Continue learning
- Annual refresher sessions for alumni
- Integration into performance management (expectations set)
- Recognition of managers implementing frameworks

## SUMMARY: PILOT TESTING FRAMEWORK

This framework allows you to validate the Manager's Field Guide before full organizational rollout.

**Timeline:** 24 weeks (6 months)
- **Weeks 1-8:** Phase 1 (Small group, tight feedback loops)
- **Weeks 9-16:** Phase 2 (Medium group, rigorous measurement)
- **Weeks 17-20:** Phase 3 (Impact assessment)
- **Weeks 21-24:** Refinement and rollout prep

**Key Principle:** Gather data in layers (observation → feedback → measurement → impact) so you can make informed decisions.

**Go/No-Go Decision:** Based on satisfaction + adoption + team impact, decide whether to roll out organization-wide or pause for revisions.

**Scaling:** If GO, use refined content and trained facilitators to roll out in phases across remaining managers.
