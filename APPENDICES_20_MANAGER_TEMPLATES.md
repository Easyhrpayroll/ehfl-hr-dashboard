## APPENDIX 6: Interview Question Bank

### HANDLING DIFFICULTY
1. "Tell me about a time you had to do something challenging. What made it hard? How did you handle it?"
2. "Tell me about a time you failed. What happened? What did you learn?"

### RELATIONSHIP BUILDING
1. "Tell me about a time you built a strong relationship quickly. How did you do it?"
2. "Tell me about a difficult person you had to work with. How did you handle it?"

### WORK STANDARDS
1. "Tell me about a time you maintained quality when under pressure."
2. "Tell me about a time you didn't meet a commitment. Why? What happened?"

### PROBLEM-SOLVING
1. "Tell me about a problem you solved. How did you approach it?"
2. "Tell me about a time you were stuck. How did you get unstuck?"

### MOTIVATION
1. "What are you looking for in your next role?"
2. "Tell me about a time you went above and beyond. Why?"

**For each answer:**
- Listen to the story
- Ask 2-3 follow-up questions
- Notice if they own their role or blame others
- Rate on your key criteria (1-5)

## APPENDIX 7: Reference Check Script

**Opening:**
"Hi [Name], I'm calling because [Candidate Name] applied for a role with us. I wanted to understand more about how they work. Do you have 10 minutes?"

**Questions:**
1. "What was [Candidate Name]'s role when you knew them?"
2. "What were they best at?"
3. "What did they struggle with? Where would they need to improve?"
4. "If you were in my shoes, would you hire them? Why or why not?"
5. "Is there anything you'd want me to know that I haven't asked?"

**What to Listen For:**
- Hesitation in answering
- Specific examples (good sign) vs. generalities (red flag)
- Energy when talking about them (positive or negative)
- Honest feedback vs. politeness

## APPENDIX 9: Onboarding Checklist (First 90 Days)

### WEEK 1: Orientation
- [ ] First day: Company policies, office tour, meet team
- [ ] Role clarity: Here's what you're accountable for
- [ ] Explain Results + Relationships culture
- [ ] Set up systems access, email, etc.
- [ ] Schedule daily check-ins

### WEEK 2-4: Learning
- [ ] Shadow top performer (3+ days)
- [ ] Observe how work actually happens
- [ ] Start practicing with supervision
- [ ] Daily check-ins: What are you learning? What's hard?
- [ ] Introduction to key stakeholders

### MONTH 2: Developing
- [ ] Gradually increase autonomy
- [ ] Weekly coaching on performance
- [ ] Give feedback on what's working and what to improve
- [ ] Introduce them to customers/stakeholders
- [ ] Get feedback from others on how they're doing

### MONTH 3: Independence
- [ ] Independent performance
- [ ] Weekly check-ins (at minimum)
- [ ] Feedback on both performance and growth
- [ ] 90-day review: How's it going?
- [ ] Set expectations for ongoing success
